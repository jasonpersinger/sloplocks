METHODOLOGY

Three independent models generate match probabilities, then blended via optimized weights:

> Dixon-Coles — Poisson-based model with attack/defense parameters for each team. Captures scoring rates and corrects for low-score dependencies.

> Expected Goals (xG) — Uses underlying shot quality data from Understat. More predictive than raw results for future performance.

> Elo Ratings — Dynamic strength ratings updated after each match. Accounts for home advantage and margin of victory.

Edge = Model Probability - Implied Probability from odds. Positive edge = potential value bet.

Powered by Dixon-Coles + xG + Elo ensemble
